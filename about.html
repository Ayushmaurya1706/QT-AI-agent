About RamanaSoft

RamanaSoft is an IT services, consulting, and software development company founded as a sister enterprise to Quality Thought by tech educator and entrepreneur Ramana Bhupathi. While Quality Thought functions primarily as the training hub, RamanaSoft operates as the commercial entity that focuses on custom product engineering, professional software consulting, and structured technology internships.

  • IT & Consulting Services: Develops custom software solutions, product engineering pipelines, and business consultancy frameworks across multiple industry domains (such as InsurTech and AI-powered code analysis tools)

  • Internships & Practical Training: Provides graduates and tech aspirants with project-based internships where they work alongside full-time engineers on live applications

  • Placement & Staffing Drives: Routinely conducts placement drives, interview preparation, and technical tracking to bridge candidate profiles into entry-level software positions
